# Dopaset
Break Free From Dopamine Overload

## Dopaset is a next-generation productivity system designed to help you regain control over your digital habits. Track your behavior, eliminate distractions, and build real discipline — one day at a time.

This is not just a tracker.
This is a system that rewires your focus.

## How It Works

Dopaset transforms your daily routine into a structured challenge:

Set your baseline (screen time & habits)
Get a personalized daily plan
Complete required and optional tasks
Earn XP, build streaks, and level up
Analyze your progress with real data

Every day is a new attempt to improve.
Consistency is everything.
